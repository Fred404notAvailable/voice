Mathematical Core
Sequential Decision Theory

Leaky SPRT Sequential Risk Engine

Why settle for simple probability averaging? VoiceSentry applies Abraham Wald's Sequential Probability Ratio Test with continuous leakage and hysteresis.

Log-Likelihood Ratio & Calibration

Raw model logits are calibrated via Temperature Scaling on held-out dev audio to guarantee reliable probabilistic confidence:

p_calibrated = σ(z / T)
Λ_t = ln [ p_cal / (1 - p_cal) ]

Each 0.5s hop produces an instantaneous Log-Likelihood Ratio (Λ_t). Positive values indicate spoof evidence; negative values indicate genuine human speech.

Leaky Accumulation

To avoid getting permanently locked on an early acoustic anomaly, evidence decays with memory factor λ = 0.98:

S_t = λ · S_{t-1} + Λ_t

Decision Thresholds & Hysteresis

Thresholds are derived from strict false-alarm (α) and false-negative (β) error budgets:

Upper Bound (Cloned): A = ln [ (1 - β) / α ]
Lower Bound (Authentic): B = ln [ β / (1 - α) ]

Hysteresis Protection: Crossing bound A triggers the CLONED state only after sustained confirmation over 2 consecutive hops (N_commit ≥ 2), eliminating false-alarm spikes from background coughs or transient line noise.

ETA Forecast Calculation:
If drift rate ΔS > 0: ETA = (A - S_t) / (ΔS / hop_s) seconds. Fraud operators see an early warning countdown before final latch!

How Grad-CAM Uncovers Deepfakes

VoiceSentry takes the gradient of the spoof logit score y^c with respect to the feature activation maps A^k of the final convolutional layer:

α_k^c = (1 / Z) · Σ_i Σ_j (∂y^c / ∂A_{i,j}^k)
L_{Grad-CAM}^c = ReLU( Σ_k α_k^c · A^k )

The resulting 2D activation matrix is interpolated across the 64 mel bins × 101 time frames, normalized into [0, 255], and packed into a lightweight base64 payload.

The frontend draws this as a translucent Inferno colormap heatmap overlay directly onto the scrolling spectrogram canvas.

What the Forensic Heatmap Reveals

  • Phase Smearing: Diffusion and neural vocoders often produce blurriness along rapid consonant transitions (e.g. /t/, /k/, /s/).
  • Unnatural Harmonic Striations: Overly uniform fundamental frequency overtones without natural vocal fold micro-tremors.
  • High-Band Glitches: Discontinuous high-frequency spectral patches where vocoders struggle to simulate vocal tract breath turbulence.

Real-World Engineering
The 8 kHz Nyquist Reality

Telephony Hardening & Codec Robustness

Academic deepfake detectors fail in real deployment because telephone networks compress, compand, and distort human voice.

The 8 kHz Nyquist Ceiling

Standard carrier audio and anti-spoofing datasets run at 16 kHz sampling. Under the Nyquist-Shannon theorem, maximum observable frequency is strictly:

f_{Nyquist} = 16,000 / 2 = 8,000 Hz

Honest Engineering Rule: Detectors claiming to identify "ultrasonic cutoff above 8 kHz" on a 16 kHz stream are peddling pseudo-science. VoiceSentry focuses its discriminative power strictly inside the 0-8 kHz band.

Our 4-8 kHz energy ratio and 95% roll-off metrics are calibrated strictly within this window.

Multi-Codec Training Augmentation

To ensure resilience against phone network distortion, our offline data pipeline creates augmented copies across telephony standards:

  • G.711 μ-law & A-law: Standard PSTN logarithmic companding (non-linear 8-bit quantization).
  • Opus 16k & 8k: VoIP, WhatsApp, and WebRTC compression with packet loss simulation.
  • Low-Bitrate MP3 (32 kbps): Sub-band spectral masking distortions.
  • Additive Acoustic Noise (20 dB SNR): Realistic call center and street background interference.
Preserves detection accuracy within 10% of clean audio

Ethics, Privacy & Anti-Goals

Voice forensics requires strict ethical boundaries. VoiceSentry is built with uncompromising privacy principles.

In-Memory Ephemeral Buffer
